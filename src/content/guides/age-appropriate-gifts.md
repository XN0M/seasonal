## Ask the grown-up who knows them

Interests, space at home, sibling ages and existing toys matter. A short conversation can prevent a duplicate or unsuitable gift.

## Use the manufacturer’s age guidance

Check the exact item’s label and safety warnings. Images in this preview are illustrative; no age claim about a real product can be inferred from them.

## Look beyond the first afternoon

Consider storage, cleaning, replacement parts and whether the activity can be enjoyed again. Do not assume every child of the same age enjoys the same play.

## Share the information with the parent

Keep instructions and relevant warnings with the gift. Adults should follow the manufacturer’s supervision and use guidance.
